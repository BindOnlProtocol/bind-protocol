Every figuresourced

  1. Same stock, several doors.

    A stock token can be swapped through more than one pool, and every pool carries a price of its own. Put them side by side and they never land in one spot.

  2. Leave out the shallow ones.

    A pool holding under 10,000 USD of liquidity is dropped. A price taken from a puddle tells you about the puddle, not about the stock.

  3. Find the middle, then measure from it.

    The median of the remaining pools becomes the centre line. Each pool is placed by how many basis points it sits above or below that centre.

  4. Name the source and let the clock run.

    Every figure names the service it came from and carries an age that counts up by the second. Past its window a figure vanishes, and in its place you read the word unread.

Four states, four shapes

Colour helps, but the shape of the dot is what you read, so the meaning holds for anyone, on any screen.

  1. Live.

    A solid dot. The figure was read inside its window: half a minute for the node, three for DefiLlama, two for DexScreener.

    inside the window

  2. Stale.

    A dot filled to its middle. The last answer is genuine but aging. It stays on screen, dimmed, with its age next to it.

    real, but aging

  3. Unread.

    A dot with nothing inside. No digit is drawn: not a placeholder, and not the last good value carried forward.

    no figure

  4. Partial.

    The bar chip counts how many of the three sources are current, so a view built on one answer never poses as a view built on three.

    n of 3 current

Questions

What does BIND do?
It reads Robinhood Chain from three public services and draws the pools of each stock token on one line, with each figure tied to the service that supplied it and to its age.
Where does the centre line come from?
From the pools themselves: it is the median price of the pools that hold at least 10,000 USD. No outside benchmark is read yet, so the page calls it the median and never a reference.
Why would a figure say unread?
The request failed, the node answered for some other chain, or the last good answer has outlived its window. A visible gap is more honest than an old number.
Can I trade here?
No. This site only reads. It has no wallet code, moves no funds and holds nothing of yours.
Is there a token?
No address is stated yet. When one is, it shows up whole in the bar at the top and copies with a click. Until then that slot does nothing.
Who may use the stock tokens?
Availability depends on where you live, plus the terms each issuer sets. Checking that is your job, and this page cannot do it for you.
