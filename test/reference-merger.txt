One share, many tokens.
Robinhood, xStocks, Ondo, Binance bStocks and Coinbase each issue a token of the same share. Each quotes its own unit, so the raw prices sit apart.
Divide out the multiplier.
Most issuers fold dividends into a unit multiplier. The book reads each multiplier from the issuer's own source and divides it out, so every row is a price per share.
Measure against one reference.
Each row is set against one Chainlink reference price and shown as a spread in basis points, with its source and age. A feed priced from a DEX pool never counts as the reference, and a row that cannot be read says unread.
Keep the wallet out of it.
Stock Tokens on Robinhood Chain can be bought from the desk, funded from shielded ZEC. The book stays public. The wallet that paid stays unlinked.

Questions
What is a merged book?
One list per share: each issuer's token of it, divided by that issuer's multiplier and measured in basis points from one reference price.
Are the tokens interchangeable?
No. Each token is its own claim, with its own issuer, terms and eligibility. The book lines them up by price per share and keeps them separate.
Which rows can I buy here?
Stock Tokens on Robinhood Chain, through Uniswap, from the desk. Rows from xStocks, Ondo, Binance bStocks and Coinbase are there for reference.
Who holds the key?
Your browser. The position account key is made in the page and kept encrypted under your passphrase. No MERGER server holds a key or your funds.
What does the rail keep public?
NEAR Intents 1Click runs here in its public mode. Each quote's record, recipient and refund address included, can be read by anyone holding its deposit address (status endpoint probed 2026-09-28 UTC).
Who can use the desk?
Stock Tokens are not offered to US persons and are restricted in Canada, the UK and Switzerland. That rule is yours to keep: do not use the desk where it applies to you.
